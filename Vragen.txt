SCORING
=======

Speed: every question starts at full points and drops linearly to half by the
time the countdown ends. So answering instantly gets 100%, at the buzzer 50%.
That speed factor is applied on top of whatever share you earned (see Scoring
in each question).

Answering closes when time is up or 2 seconds after everyone has answered.

Rankings are shown after every 5th question; the last question goes straight
to the final results.
