SCORING
=======

Speed: every question starts at full points and drops linearly to half by the
time the countdown ends. So answering instantly gets 100%, at the buzzer 50%.
That speed factor is applied on top of whatever share you earned (see Scoring
in each question).

Answering closes when time is up or 2 seconds after everyone has answered.

Rankings are shown after every 5th question; the last question goes straight
to the final results.

(
  What is the secret ingredient in Kyle's Red Wine Spaghetti?

  Open question: everyone types an answer, Kyle too. Whatever Kyle typed is the
  right answer; it's shown on the big screen at the reveal ("Kyle says it's..."),
  with Ruben's answer and how many got it right. Other answers stay private.
  Everyone, Ruben included, is scored against Kyle's answer.
  If Kyle didn't answer, the host types in the answer instead.

  Scoring: 1500 pts, 45s. Full points when the answer matches Kyle's, allowing for
  case, accents, punctuation, plurals, small typos, filler words ("the", "some", ...)
  and extra words around it ("I think it's cinnamon"). No partial points.
  Kyle himself gets no points for this one.
)
